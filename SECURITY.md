# Reporting exploits

Found a way to cheat (rig dice, fake a win, dodge a wager) or to break or
spam another player's game? Please **don't post it publicly**. Use
[private reporting](https://github.com/XOData/TheHearth-releases/security/advisories/new)
(Security tab → Report a vulnerability). Include the version (`/hearth`
shows it), what you did, and what the other player saw.
